About Us

Bag Bag is an online shopping store which is successfully
manufacturing and exporting leather
products since 2012. We at Bag Bag
are putting our utmost efforts in
providing you high-quality and
environmentally friendly leather
products. We aimed to
manufacture products that last
longer without harming the env-
ironment. We are the manufactures
and exporters of handbags, Foot wears,
shoulder straps, wallets, jewelry, and other accessories. Our goal is to produce quality bags and wallets using innovative and sustainable materials such as Vegan leather. It is a type of leather made from pineapple leaves, recycled plastic, apple peels and other fruit waste. Welcome to Bag Bag and shop for stylish handbags and wallets designed with beautiful attractive colors and styles.
